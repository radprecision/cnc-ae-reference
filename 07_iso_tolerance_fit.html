Reading a tolerance class: H7Letter (uppercase) = hole, fundamental deviation. H = lower deviation is zero (hole starts at nominal, goes up only). Number = IT grade (tolerance band width). Lower IT = tighter. H7 = precision reaming/boring. g6 = shaft paired for sliding fit.

All H holes: EI = 0.000The hole lower limit is always exactly the nominal diameter. Upper limit = nominal + IT grade value. The fit type is determined by the shaft letter (g, k, p…) paired with the H hole.
Clearance fit
Shaft always smaller than hole. Part rotates or slides freely. H7/g6, H8/f7, H9/d9
Transition fit
Can be clearance or interference depending on tolerance extremes. H7/k6, H7/n6
Interference fit
Shaft always larger than hole. Must press or heat to assemble. H7/p6, H7/s6
All H holes: EI (lower deviation) = 0.000mm. ES (upper deviation) = value shown. Tolerance = ES − EI = value shown.
