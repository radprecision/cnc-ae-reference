🔬 ISO Material Classification

CNC AE Reference Library — Module 10 · ISCAR 41-group system · P / M / K / N / S / H

ISO 513 material classification — six groups, 41 subgroupsEvery workpiece material maps to one of six ISO groups. The group determines the insert grade family (P, M, K, N, S, H), the coating strategy, and the starting SFM range. Subgroup numbers are manufacturer-specific — ISCAR uses 1–41, Sandvik uses a different CMC code system.

Subgroup numbers are manufacturer-specificISCAR, Sandvik, Kennametal, and Walter all use different subgroup numbering systems within the six ISO groups. The letter (P, M, K, N, S, H) is universal. The number after the letter is not — always verify with the specific manufacturer's catalog.

ISO S — Super alloys (brown)Highest cutting forces, lowest thermal conductivity, maximum work hardening tendency. The most demanding material group to machine. Rigid setup, correct tooling, and high-pressure coolant are non-negotiable.
SubgroupMaterialExamplesKey challengeApproach
S31–S32Iron-base super alloysA286, Incoloy 800, 17-4PH agedHigh strength, moderate work hardeningPVD TiAlN, flood coolant, moderate SFM, rigid setup
S33–S35Nickel/cobalt-base super alloysInconel 718, Hastelloy C276, Waspaloy, Rene 41Extreme cutting forces, very low thermal conductivity — heat stays in toolPVD TiAlN or AlTiN. High pressure TSC essential. Ceramic for dry roughing (no interruptions). Rigid setup mandatory.
S36Titanium — commercially pureCP Ti Grade 1–4Work hardening, fire risk above 600°C, galling on steel toolingPVD TiAlN, flood coolant always, non-ferrous contact pads for workholding
S37Titanium alloysTi-6Al-4V, Ti-6Al-2Sn-4Zr-2Mo, Ti-3Al-2.5VSame as CP Ti but higher strength — worst-case Ti for machiningPVD TiAlN, TSC 1000+ psi for drilling, high positive rake, no ceramic (fire risk)
Ceramic inserts for Inconel roughing must run DRY at high SFM with zero interruptions. Coolant causes thermal shock and shatters the insert. Titanium must NEVER be run dry — fire risk above 600°C.
ISO H — Hardened materials (gray)Steel hardened above 45 HRC, hardened cast iron, chilled cast iron. CBN (cubic boron nitride) is the primary cutting material. Hard turning with CBN can replace cylindrical grinding when conditions are met.
