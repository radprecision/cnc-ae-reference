〰 Surface Finish Reference

CNC AE Reference Library — Module 17 · Dual units throughout (µm metric + µin inch)

Ra alone is insufficient for critical surfaces. A bearing surface needs Rsk (skewness) — negative skewness means valleys that hold oil. A sealing surface needs Rz. Always specify the parameter that matches the function.
ParameterNameWhat it measuresWhen to specify it
RaArithmetic mean roughnessAverage deviation of profile from mean line. The most common parameter.General surface quality, most machined surfaces. Default specification.
RzMean peak-to-valley heightAverage of the 5 largest peak-to-valley distances in the evaluation length.Sealing surfaces, gasket faces. More sensitive to spikes than Ra.
Rq / RMSRoot mean square roughnessRMS of profile deviations. More sensitive to peaks/valleys than Ra.Optical surfaces, precision fits. RMS ≈ 1.11 × Ra for random surfaces.
RtTotal profile heightDistance between highest peak and deepest valley in entire evaluation length.Fatigue-critical surfaces — single deep scratch can initiate crack.
Rp / RvMax peak / Max valley depthHighest point above / deepest point below mean line.Coating adhesion (Rp), lubrication retention (Rv).
RskSkewnessAsymmetry of profile. Negative = more valleys than peaks (plateau surface).Bearing and lubrication surfaces. Negative Rsk preferred — valleys hold oil.
Conversion shortcut: µm × 40 ≈ µin. Key pairs to memorize: 0.8/32, 1.6/63, 3.2/125, 6.3/250. Ra 3.2 µm / 125 µin / N8 = standard as-machined default.

The cost jump at Ra 0.4 µm (16 µin) is non-linear — it triggers a grinding operation. Every step tighter than Ra 3.2 adds cost. Tighter-than-necessary callouts on non-functional surfaces are pure waste.

Stylus profilometer is the reference method for all production inspection. Cut-off length (λc) must match the surface — standard λc = 0.8mm for Ra 0.1–2 µm. Measure perpendicular to lay (machining direction) for most surfaces.
