Always spot drill before drilling on curved or angled surfacesA standard drill will walk on a curved entry surface. A spot drill (90° or 120°) creates a flat starting point. On CNC with rigid fixturing, spot drilling is often omitted — but should always be used when entry geometry is not perpendicular to the drill.
Tap drill sizes at 75% thread (cut tap) and 65% thread (form tap)Form tap produces stronger thread and no chips — preferred for aluminum and blind holes. Form tap requires 5–10% larger drill than cut tap.

Reaming — the three critical rules1. Stock per side = 1–3% of diameter. 2. Speed = 50–65% of drill SFM. 3. Feed = 2–3× drill IPR (IPR = chip load × flutes for reaming). Never reverse a reamer — always flood coolant.
